Research Labs and Facilities

The Computer Engineering Department at Marmara University provides state-of-the-art research laboratories and facilities for students and faculty members.

Research Laboratories:

1. Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning Lab
   - Location: Room C-201
   - Equipment: High-performance GPU servers, Deep Learning workstations
   - Research Focus: Neural networks, deep learning, reinforcement learning
   - Lab Coordinator: Prof. Dr. Ahmet Yılmaz
   - Available for: Graduate students, undergraduate final year projects

2. Software Engineering Lab
   - Location: Room C-202
   - Equipment: Development workstations, version control systems, CI/CD tools
   - Research Focus: Software development methodologies, testing, quality assurance
   - Lab Coordinator: Assoc. Prof. Dr. Ayşe Çelik
   - Available for: All students for coursework and projects

3. Computer Networks and Security Lab
   - Location: Room C-203
   - Equipment: Network routers, switches, firewalls, security testing tools
   - Research Focus: Network protocols, cybersecurity, penetration testing
   - Lab Coordinator: Prof. Dr. Mehmet Kaya
   - Available for: Graduate students and specific coursework

4. Database and Big Data Lab
   - Location: Room C-204
   - Equipment: Database servers, Hadoop cluster, data analytics tools
   - Research Focus: Database design, big data processing, data mining
   - Lab Coordinator: Prof. Dr. Elif Demir
   - Available for: Graduate students and database courses

5. Computer Vision Lab
   - Location: Room C-205
   - Equipment: High-resolution cameras, image processing workstations
   - Research Focus: Image processing, pattern recognition, computer vision
   - Lab Coordinator: Assoc. Prof. Dr. Can Özkan
   - Available for: Graduate students and related coursework

6. Embedded Systems and IoT Lab
   - Location: Room C-206
   - Equipment: Arduino boards, Raspberry Pi, sensors, IoT devices
   - Research Focus: Embedded programming, IoT applications, sensor networks
   - Lab Coordinator: Asst. Prof. Dr. Burak Yıldız
   - Available for: All students for hands-on projects

General Computer Laboratories:

Lab A: 40 workstations - General programming and coursework
Lab B: 30 workstations - Operating systems and system programming
Lab C: 35 workstations - Web development and databases
Lab D: 25 workstations - Mobile application development

Operating Hours:
Monday - Friday: 08:00 - 20:00
Saturday: 09:00 - 17:00
Sunday: Closed

Lab Access:
Students must have their student ID cards to access the laboratories. Research labs require prior permission from the lab coordinator. General labs are open to all students during operating hours.

Available Software:
- IDEs: Visual Studio, Eclipse, IntelliJ IDEA, PyCharm
- Programming Languages: Python, Java, C/C++, JavaScript, C#
- Database Systems: MySQL, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Oracle
- Development Tools: Git, Docker, Kubernetes
- Machine Learning: TensorFlow, PyTorch, Scikit-learn
- Data Analytics: MATLAB, R, Apache Spark

Library and Resources:
The department has a dedicated technical library with computer science books, journals, and online resources. Students have access to ACM Digital Library, IEEE Xplore, and other academic databases.

Technical Support:
IT support staff are available Monday-Friday from 09:00-17:00 for technical issues. Students can submit support tickets through the department website or visit the IT office in Room C-101.
