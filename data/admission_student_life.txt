Admission and Student Life

Admission Requirements:

For Turkish Students:
The Computer Engineering Department at Marmara University accepts students through the YKS (Yükseköğretim Kurumları Sınavı) central university entrance examination. Students must:
- Take the SAY (Numerical) section of YKS
- Meet the minimum score threshold announced each year (typically around 400-420 points)
- Submit preferences through ÖSYM system during the placement period

For International Students:
International applicants can apply through:
- YÖS (Foreign Student Examination) organized by Marmara University
- SAT or equivalent international examination scores
- High school diploma with transcript
- English proficiency certificate (TOEFL, IELTS, or equivalent)

Application Period:
- Turkish Students: July-August (following YKS results)
- International Students: May-June for Fall semester

Student Support Services:

Academic Advising:
Each student is assigned a faculty advisor who provides guidance on:
- Course selection and academic planning
- Career development and internship opportunities
- Graduate school applications
- Research project opportunities

Career Services:
The department maintains strong industry connections and helps students with:
- Internship placements (30-day mandatory internship)
- Job fairs and recruitment events
- Resume and interview preparation
- Alumni networking events

Scholarships and Financial Aid:
Available scholarships include:
- Merit-based scholarships for high achievers
- Need-based financial aid
- Research assistantship positions
- Industry-sponsored scholarships

International Programs:

Erasmus+ Exchange Program:
Students can study abroad for one or two semesters at partner universities in Europe. Partner institutions include universities in Germany, Spain, Poland, and other EU countries.

Mevlana Exchange Program:
Exchange program with universities across the world, including institutions in Asia, Middle East, and Americas.

Exchange Requirements:
- Completed at least 2 semesters
- Minimum GPA of 2.5
- Language proficiency in the host country's language
