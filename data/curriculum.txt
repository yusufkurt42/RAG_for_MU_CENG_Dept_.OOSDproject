Undergraduate Program Curriculum - Computer Engineering

The undergraduate program at Marmara University Computer Engineering Department consists of 8 semesters with a total of 240 ECTS credits.

First Year Courses:
Fall Semester:
- Introduction to Computer Engineering and Programming I
- Calculus I
- Physics I
- Chemistry
- Technical English I
- Atatürk's Principles and History of Turkish Revolution I

Spring Semester:
- Introduction to Computer Engineering and Programming II
- Calculus II
- Physics II
- Linear Algebra
- Technical English II
- Atatürk's Principles and History of Turkish Revolution II

Second Year Courses:
Fall Semester:
- Data Structures
- Discrete Mathematics
- Digital Logic Design
- Differential Equations
- Object-Oriented Programming

Spring Semester:
- Algorithms and Complexity
- Computer Organization
- Database Systems
- Probability and Statistics
- System Programming

Third Year Courses:
Fall Semester:
- Operating Systems
- Computer Networks
- Software Engineering
- Microprocessor Systems
- Elective I

Spring Semester:
- Artificial Intelligence
- Computer Architecture
- Web Technologies
- Numerical Analysis
- Elective II

Fourth Year Courses:
Fall Semester:
- Compiler Design
- Computer Security
- Elective III
- Elective IV
- Graduation Project I

Spring Semester:
- Professional Ethics
- Entrepreneurship
- Elective V
- Elective VI
- Graduation Project II

Elective Courses Include:
- Machine Learning
- Deep Learning
- Computer Vision
- Natural Language Processing
- Mobile Application Development
- Cloud Computing
- Internet of Things
- Big Data Analytics
- Blockchain Technologies
- Game Development
- Computer Graphics
- Distributed Systems
- Software Testing and Quality Assurance
- Human-Computer Interaction

Graduation Requirements:
- Complete all required courses with a minimum GPA of 2.0
- Complete 30 days of mandatory internship
- Successfully defend graduation project
- Total of 240 ECTS credits

Students must maintain a minimum GPA of 2.0 to graduate. The program includes both theoretical courses and practical laboratory work to ensure students gain hands-on experience.
